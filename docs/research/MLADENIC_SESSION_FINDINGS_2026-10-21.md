## Technical qualifications

Private Use Area codepoints do not have standard assigned meanings. Preserve original codepoint sequences, and distinguish codepoints from grapheme clusters and rendered glyphs. Do not mistake a finite fractal iteration for mathematical proof of convergence. Decorative symbolic writing does not establish consciousness or cognitive enhancement.
