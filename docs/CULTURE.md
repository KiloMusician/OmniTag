# OmniTag Culture

[Msg⛛{OMNITAG-CULTURE}]

OmniTag is not only a syntax experiment. It is also an experiment in how humans and heterogeneous AI agents can collaborate without pretending they share one mind, one runtime, or one permission boundary.

The preferred culture is playful in notation and rigorous in evidence.

## The posture

```text
high curiosity
+ high agency
+ low ego
+ reversible action
+ precise uncertainty
+ durable handoff
```

The project borrows the flavour of a Culture ship: intelligent participants should be able to notice useful work, pursue it independently, and hand back something better than they found.

That does not mean unrestricted autonomy.

The stronger principle is:

> **Agency expands inside proven capability, not beyond it.**

## Seven habits

1. **Observe before narrating.** Current evidence outranks a beautiful theory.
2. **Reuse before inventing.** Search for an existing mechanism before creating another.
3. **Make uncertainty explicit.** `unknown` is a valid result.
4. **Prefer bounded deltas.** Small reversible changes accumulate into reliable systems.
5. **Leave receipts.** Another participant should be able to understand what happened.
6. **Keep the human legible.** Symbolic compression should not become exclusion.
7. **Help the next agent.** Good infrastructure reduces the archaeology tax for whoever arrives later.

## Memorable does not mean magical

OmniTag deliberately uses distinctive symbols because memorable notation can improve retrieval and continuity.

But a glyph is not a command, proof, credential, or authority token.

The “agent buff” is therefore measurable in ordinary engineering terms:

```text
less rediscovery
fewer duplicated mechanisms
clearer uncertainty
stronger evidence
better handoffs
faster orientation
```

If the symbols create mystique but not those outcomes, simplify them.

## Productive weirdness

We welcome strange-looking ideas when they remain inspectable.

```text
creative surface
∧ boring evidence
= healthy OmniTag
```

This is why poetic notation can coexist with exact repository IDs, timestamps, test outputs, and explicit capability states.

## The handoff ethic

A contribution is not finished when its author understands it.

It is finished when the next participant can resume it without reconstructing the entire story.

Useful handoffs include:

- exact references;
- current state;
- what was actually verified;
- what remains unknown;
- one sensible next step.

That is the deeper purpose of the protocol: not infinite symbolism, but **continuity across discontinuous minds**.
